About Us

Wayfinder is a small travel company that helps people choose where to go next. We turn big travel questions into short, honest recommendations.

Who we are

Wayfinder was founded in 2020 by a group of friends who were tired of endless lists of "top ten" destinations. We believe a good recommendation is specific, current and written by someone who has actually been there.

Today our team of travel writers, photographers and developers maintains recommendations for beaches, temples and cities on every continent.

Our mission

Help every traveller find a place that fits them, and make planning the trip as enjoyable as taking it.

Guidance for team members

  • Be accurate. Check names, locations and facts against at least two sources before publishing a recommendation.
  • Be specific. Say what makes a place worth visiting in one or two clear sentences.
  • Use real images. Every recommendation needs at least one clear photo with descriptive alt text.
  • Respect local culture. Describe temples and sacred sites with care, including dress codes and photography rules.
  • Reply fast. Answer messages from the Contact Us form within two working days.
